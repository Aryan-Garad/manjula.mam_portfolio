Biography

Who is Manjula Subhash Nair?

Manjula Subhash Nair is one of India's foremost authorities on marketing, brand strategy, and corporate communications — a professional with more than 25 years of experience navigating some of the most complex business environments in the technology and services sectors.

Her career spans senior leadership roles at global organisations including Hewlett Packard, Sapient, Satyam Computers, and Amdocs — institutions where she honed a distinctive ability to align marketing strategy with business outcomes at scale. Her involvement during the Satyam crisis period stands as a testament to her expertise in crisis communications and corporate reputation management under exceptional pressure.

Driven by a conviction that marketing's highest purpose is to create sustainable business growth, Manjula transitioned from corporate leadership to entrepreneurship — founding Global CMO Partners and Creative Bharat to serve startups, SMEs, and enterprises seeking strategic marketing counsel and communications expertise.

Alongside her consulting work, she serves as a Professor of Practice teaching Entrepreneurship & Innovation at COEP's Bhau Institute and NICMAR University, bringing real-world marketing rigour into academic programmes. She is also a recognised mentor at Stanford SEED and regularly contributes to industry discourse as a speaker and panelist at national conferences and innovation summits.
